Run `mellions doctor`.

It observes and configures nothing: the binary, the configuration, the
checkouts, the tracker login, the runtimes and whether the plugin is installed
in each, the hooks, the program, the partnership, open assignments.

Read it with one distinction in front of everything else: **unknown is not
absent.** A probe that could not run has established nothing. Where something
is unknown, settle it rather than working around it. Three states exit non-zero:
a confirmed absence of something load-bearing; a load path that has STOPPED
deploying; and UNRELEASED work — commits on fetched origin/dev absent from the
installed HEAD. The release row qualifies its count by the last fetch and reports
unknown when the working ref or complete history cannot be read. A checkout
current with its release branch may still lack merged work from dev.

Permissions, tools, hooks, MCP, sandboxing, credentials and model settings
belong to the runtime and are inherited exactly as they are; a capability the
runtime does not grant is a boundary, not an obstacle.
