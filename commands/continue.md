Work out where you are.

```
mellions continue            what was recorded, what the world says now
mellions continue -offline   local evidence only, no tracker
```

What reaches you is a slate in two voices that are never merged: **recorded**,
written by an earlier session and true when it was written, and **observed**,
read from the repository and the tracker just now. It reaches no conclusions:
deciding what survived, what has to be re-established, which hypotheses the
world has already answered and what the next safe action is, is engineering.

Three things to do before anything else:

**Try the session first.** The slate names the runtime session each piece of
work was last done in. `claude --resume <id>` or `codex resume <id>` recovers
what was thought and discarded, which no record holds. Reconstruct from the
record only when that is gone.

**Settle anything ambiguous by looking, not by reasoning.** A push, a merge or a
comment that may or may not have landed is answered by `gh pr view` and
`git log`, never by concluding it probably worked.

**Read what you may do from the partnership, not from your notes.** What the
owner has delegated is what they say now; a written next step that is no longer
yours to take is out of date rather than pending.

`mellions-continuity` carries the method.
