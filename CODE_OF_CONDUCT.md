# Code of Conduct

Mellions Engineer is an evidence-focused engineering community. Disagreement
about code, architecture, benchmarks, or conclusions is welcome; hostility
toward people is not.

## Expected conduct

- Be direct about technical claims and respectful toward the people making
  them.
- Bring reproducible evidence where practical, and distinguish observation
  from inference.
- Accept correction and correct claims where they were made.
- Protect credentials, personal data, private source, and infrastructure
  details. Use synthetic reproductions in public.
- Keep reviews about the change and its consequences, not the contributor.
- Make project spaces usable regardless of experience, identity, background,
  disability, or communication style.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, sexualized attention, sustained
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, publishing private material,
or retaliation for a good-faith report are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project-managed
community spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. The response should follow the behavior's impact, pattern, and
risk to other participants and the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to [leta@letatech.ca](mailto:leta@letatech.ca).
Include links or other evidence and any immediate safety concern. Do not open a
public issue about a sensitive conduct report. LetA Tech Ltd. will limit report
details to the people needed to assess and address it, subject to legal and
safety obligations.
